## 🧭 Why

During a disaster, reports and tools appear across many agencies, and most people do not know where to find them. This hub gathers those links in one place for students, researchers, citizens, and volunteers.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The hub does **not** generate data. It indexes official and community sources. Every link belongs to the organisation that publishes it.

---

## 🗂️ What's indexed

| Section | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Scientific & Environmental Data** | Department of Meteorology, NBRO landslide warnings, Irrigation Department river and reservoir data |
| **Satellite Intelligence & Map Visualization** | Vantor/Maxar open imagery, Copernicus EMS, International Charter, ESA, Navy inundation maps |
| **Real-Time Response Tools** | Community platforms for SOS, damage maps, missing persons, river alerts, tourist assistance |
| **Crisis Management & Statistics** | DMC SitReps, Tri-Forces operations, Treasury and verified fundraising links |
| **International & Humanitarian Data** | ReliefWeb (UN OCHA), Sri Lanka Red Cross |
| **Global Analysis & Strategic Insights** | World Bank, WMO, IWMI, and other verified commentary |

The page also has search across entries and a dark mode that remembers your choice in the browser.

## 🔁 How it works

A single [`index.html`](index.html), styled with Tailwind CSS from its CDN and icons from Font Awesome, served by GitHub Pages from `main`. There is no build step, backend, or API key.

---

## 🚀 Getting started

Open the [live site](https://bronxtj.github.io/Sri-Lanka-Disaster-Data-Resource-Hub/), or:

```bash
git clone https://github.com/BronXTJ/Sri-Lanka-Disaster-Data-Resource-Hub.git
cd Sri-Lanka-Disaster-Data-Resource-Hub
```

Then open `index.html` in a browser. An internet connection is needed for styles, icons, and the linked sources.

---

## ➕ Adding a source

Use **Know a Verified Source?** on the site, which opens a Google Form. Feedback goes through **Share Your Thoughts**. Submissions are reviewed before they appear.

---

## ⚠️ Limitations

- **Links are curated by hand.** The page shows "Last updated: Dec 2025"; a link can move or go offline after that.
- **Not an emergency service.** For urgent help, contact official channels such as the DMC or police.
- **Needs internet.** Styles and icons load from CDNs.
